8. Accuracy of recognition

Lanyard reads printed cards using on-device text recognition and then applies rules to decide which line is which. It is frequently exactly right and it is sometimes wrong: unusual layouts, small or stylised type, glare, damaged cards and names that do not follow the conventions the app knows about will all produce mistakes.

Check anything that matters before relying on it. The app highlights the fields it was unsure about and shows the reasoning behind each one so you can check its work, but an unhighlighted field is a confident guess rather than a guarantee. A misdialled number or a misspelled name is your responsibility to catch.
